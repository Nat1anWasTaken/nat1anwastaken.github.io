Hi, I'm Nat1an. I'm a computer science student in Taiwan, a percussionist, and an organiser at [SITCON](https://sitcon.org). Until now this site only showed my [work](/#work). From now on it also has a place to write.

## What to expect

I'll write about the things I spend my time on:

- **Building software.** What I learned building a project, and what I'd do differently.
- **Building communities.** How student conferences and camps like SITCON come together behind the scenes.
- **Everything else.** Music, school, and whatever else I can't stop thinking about.

Posts will be in English, Chinese, or both. This one is also [in Chinese](/blog/hello-world-zh/).

## Following along

There's an [RSS feed](/rss.xml) if you use a feed reader. If something here is useful or wrong, [let me know](/#contact).
